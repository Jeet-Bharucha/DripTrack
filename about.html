Our approach

Good information.
Honest boundaries.

DripTrack helps you discover listings for sneakers, watches, bags and streetwear. We don’t sell products, fulfill orders or independently authenticate items.

Where the listings come from

Our server requests listing data from the eBay Browse API, Amazon search through Rainforest, and Google Shopping through SerpAPI. A configured key does not mean a provider is working. The status below reflects the latest request made by this running server.

Checking provider status…

Google Shopping results are an index, not a direct inventory feed. A “Shopping result” link opens the provider’s product page; a “Seller listing” link opens the URL the provider supplied. We do not substitute a generic retailer search URL for a priced listing.

What a price means

Prices are reported in US dollars by the data provider. Search responses with results are cached for up to one hour. “Fetched” is when our server received the data, not a promise that the seller updated it then. The seller’s final price, availability, shipping and taxes may differ.

We retain the source’s product title, image, condition and description when supplied. Missing images or descriptions are shown as unavailable. Different sizes, colorways, sellers and conditions are separate listings; the cheapest search result is not necessarily a comparable deal.

Authenticity is a seller question

DripTrack does not certify products or sellers. A brand name, seller feedback score or price is not proof of authenticity. Review the seller, condition, return policy and any marketplace authentication program before purchasing.

History without guesswork

We record a price observation when an uncached search returns that exact source listing. History is keyed to the listing, not a mixture of search results. We never generate estimated charts, fake movements or backfilled prices. Early listings may have just one observation.

Alerts and availability

Price alerts check the exact saved listing when this server is running, approximately every 30 minutes. A match is required before an alert can trigger. Checks can be delayed by provider outages, credit limits or hosting sleep. Verify your email before enabling alerts. Alerts rearm after an observed price rises above the target. You can edit or pause them from your account. Email delivery is not guaranteed. Existing alerts without a listing ID need to be recreated from a current result.

Your account and data

Accounts store your name, email, password hash, saved listings and alert preferences in our database. Google sign-in also stores your Google account ID and optional avatar. Comparison selections and recent searches are held in session storage for this browser tab. Passwords are hashed. Sign-in uses an HttpOnly cookie that page scripts cannot read. Sessions can be revoked from your account; sign out on shared devices.

Search terms are sent to the listing providers named above. Product images load from their original hosts. The site uses Google Fonts. Following an external listing takes you to a third party with its own privacy and purchase terms.

You can remove saved listings and alerts, or delete your account from the account page. Deleting your account removes its profile, saved listings, alerts and push subscriptions; previously collected listing observations do not identify your account.

Questions or problems?

Report a problem on GitHub. Please do not include passwords or private connection strings in a public report.

Use of the service

DripTrack is provided as a price discovery tool. Confirm all purchase details with the seller. Retailer names identify listing sources and do not imply partnership or endorsement. DripTrack does not collect payment for purchases or control third-party listings.
